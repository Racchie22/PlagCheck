## Sprint outline (provisional)
- Sprint 1 – Working text comparison: input, preprocessing, similarity score and core tests.
- Sprint 2 – Explainable results: sentence-level matching and validation.
- Sprint 3 – Document workflow: file extraction, local document collection and persistence.
- Sprint 4 – Usability and reporting: dashboard and downloadable report.
- Sprint 5 – Stabilisation: broader tests, refactoring, CI and final documentation.

The outline is provisional. Sprint duration and actual dates will be set based on the team's remaining time. Do not report planned work as completed work.
